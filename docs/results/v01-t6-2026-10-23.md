# V01 T6 — inconclusive share-creation observation

Historical result: the observations below remain unchanged. A later
[iPad diagnosis and controlled fix](v01-t6-ipad-diagnosis-2026-10-24.md) identified
a laboratory observer-delivery deadlock in the reproduced path and observed
successful writer/fresh-store reader stages on the same iPad. That follow-up
does not convert these unfinished reports into completed trials.
A subsequent [independent iPhone reader](v01-t6-independent-reader-2026-10-24.md)
also recovered the fixed iPad writer's exact fixture and share identities.

Classification: **inconclusive**. Neither writer attempt retained a completed
callback result for creation of share A before the process ended. The clean repeat
remained pending for more than five minutes after its pre-share checkpoint and
was then explicitly terminated. This is neither a demonstrated native rejection
nor successful share/zone separation. No reader was launched.

## Interpretation and retained evidence

[Apple's API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coredata/nspersistentcloudkitcontainer/sharemanagedobjects%3Atoshare%3Acompletion%3A)
documents creation of a new share when the existing share is nil; its callback
concerns creation/assignment rather than necessarily completed object export.
That documented mechanism remains distinct from this incomplete observation.
The retained checkpoint does not establish whether any remote side effect had
already occurred while the callback was pending. No unsupported-configuration,
general API failure, separate-zone success, or selective-download conclusion
follows from these attempts. No workaround or architecture decision is selected.
