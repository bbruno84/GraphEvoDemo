independent reader on a second physical device, without local insertion or

## Interpretation and cleanup

A fresh independent-device reader recovered the full expected fixture with no
local save. Together with the explicitly confirmed device/account conditions,
this is observed T0 mirroring control evidence. A successful export event or a
record mapping alone would not establish this result.

The test used one writer store, not the T1 two-store configuration. It does not
show selective recovery of A/B, arbitrary zone control, sharing, different
accounts, relaunch resilience across every lifecycle, production behavior or
any universal timing/performance guarantee. It makes no architectural decision
about R01. Future topology trials require separate authorization and review.

After report copy and comparison, the coordinator explicitly terminated both
app processes. Console sessions exited with SIGTERM as intended; this is not a
stage failure. No remote wipe or local store deletion was performed. Synthetic
fixture records and local laboratory stores/reports remain for review. Local store detachment is recorded; remote
cleanup is explicitly pending, not implied by local closure. Any future remote
cleanup remains confined to the explicitly authorized Development resource.
